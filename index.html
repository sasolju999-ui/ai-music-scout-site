PERSONAL DJ MUSIC DISCOVERY TOOL

AI Music Scout

AI Music Scout is an independently developed music-discovery tool used to find new Slovenian, Croatian, Balkan and ex-Yugoslav music for DJ listening and evaluation.

What the application does

The application uses YouTube API Services to discover recently published music videos, filter out unsuitable items such as live performances and interviews, prepare discovery reports, and—with the account owner's permission—add eligible videos to a private YouTube playlist for later listening. The owner chooses which songs to keep.

Why it requests Google and YouTube access

The owner authorizes the application to identify the connected YouTube channel, read playlist information to avoid duplicate items, and add selected video references to the account's playlist. It does not require the account password.
